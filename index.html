🛡️ About CyberCare

CyberCare is designed to make digital safety easier to understand for ordinary users.

It provides general educational guidance for account security, scams, harassment, privacy, financial fraud and other common digital safety problems.

CyberCare is not a police service, bank, social-media company or emergency response service.
