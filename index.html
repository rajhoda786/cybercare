Emergency Cyber Help

Act quickly when money, account or personal safety is at risk.

💳 Money / UPI Fraud

  1. Contact your bank immediately.
  2. Block or secure the affected payment method.
  3. Save transaction IDs and screenshots.
  4. In India, report financial cyber fraud quickly through the official channels.

🔐 Account Hacked

  1. Secure your email first.
  2. Change the compromised password.
  3. Sign out unknown sessions.
  4. Enable 2FA.
  5. Use the platform's official recovery process.

📸 Harassment / Blackmail

  1. Do not send more money or sensitive material.
  2. Preserve evidence.
  3. Block/report the account when safe.
  4. Use official law-enforcement/cybercrime reporting routes.

Women's Digital Safety

Harassment, stalking, blackmail and privacy protection

🚨 Online Harassment

Save messages, profile URLs, usernames, dates and screenshots before deleting anything.

👁️ Digital Stalking

Review account sessions, location sharing, connected devices and app permissions.

📸 Private Image Threat / Blackmail

Do not negotiate blindly or send additional material. Preserve evidence and use official reporting routes.

🔐 Privacy Protection

Use strong unique passwords, 2FA and review who can see your personal information.
